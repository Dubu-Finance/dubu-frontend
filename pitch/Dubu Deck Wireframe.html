The structural problem
  • Most new chains cannot absorb even a $100K trade

Why liquidity never arrives
  • No mechanism exists to build tradable depth in the first place
  • Impermanent loss makes LP economics negative → no organic inflow
  • No chain has ever passively recovered liquidity once it left

Why both are required

A Prop AMM alone never reaches anyone; an Aggregator alone has no good price to hand out.

What each side does
  • Prop AMM (core product): CEX-grade quotes off a small inventory (≤0.06%)
  • Aggregator: the chain's single entry point, best-route selection, new-user onboarding
  • Compounding effect: quote competition with DEXs and Prop AMMs that follow → spread compression (the 30bp→5bp path observed on Solana)
